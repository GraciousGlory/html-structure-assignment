Liverpool vs. Brighton LIVE: Premier League updates as fifth meets third at Anfield

Liverpool welcome Brighton to Anfield as both teams compete for important Premier League points.

It should be an entertaining affair on Merseyside, but the players will be expecting heavy winds due to Storm Boelo, with gusts north of 50mph projected for the region.

Andoni Iraola's men return to domestic action on the back of a thrilling 4-3 victory over Villarreal in the Champions League, having recovered from a two-goal deficit to secure all three points in midweek. Alexis Mac Allister struck twice, with Ronald Araujo and Florian Wirtz also on target, while 18-year-old Rio Ngumoha provided two assists.

That result extended Liverpool's winning run to three matches in all competitions, following a 2-1 Premier League victory over Brentford where Alexander Isak got his fifth of the season in the top flight.

Brighton, however, will be looking to quickly move past an uninspiring goalless draw with Jablonec in the UEFA Conference League on Thursday.

Fabian Hürzeler would much prefer his team play as they did last weekend, when they delivered a remarkable 5-0 demolition of bitter rivals Crystal Palace, a result which saw the Seagulls score three times inside the opening 10 minutes. Brighton took just four minutes to break the deadlock through a Jaydee Canvot own goal, before Diego Gómez produced an extraordinary contender for goal of the season.

Having spotted Palace goalkeeper Walter Benítez off his line, the Paraguay international unleashed an audacious effort from just inside the opposition half, sending the ball sailing into the net from approximately 51 metres.

A Brighton win today would keep them within two points of Manchester City and Arsenal.

Match Information

  • Home Team: Liverpool
  • Away Team: Brighton
  • Stadium: Anfield
